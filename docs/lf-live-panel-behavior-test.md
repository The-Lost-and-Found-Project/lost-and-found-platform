# L&F Live facilitator panel test

Before merge, verify the preview build and these behaviors:

- Enter Gather: no private panel.
- Enter Teach: no private panel opens automatically.
- Open Notes: panel appears; click Notes again: panel closes.
- Open Tools or Prayer: only the selected panel appears.
- Switch Gather/Teach/Discuss: private panel closes.
- With a panel open, advance to a slide with no content for that panel: panel closes.
- With a panel open, advance to a slide that has content for that panel: panel remains available.
- Mic and camera state do not change during any mode, slide, or panel navigation.
- Full Screen still enters/exits without changing Daily media or study state.
