# L&F Live UI principles

The Bible study is the meeting.

The participant experience should prioritize Scripture, people, and the current moment. Facilitator controls should be available without becoming the visual center of the room. Controls appear when they are useful and get out of the way when they are not.

The room should feel calm rather than technical: fewer persistent panels, fewer competing surfaces, clear mode changes, stable media, and intentional use of navy, cream, and gold.
