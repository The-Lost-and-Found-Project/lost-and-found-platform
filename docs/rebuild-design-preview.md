# L&F rebuild design preview

This first implementation package adds `/rebuild-preview` as a public, read-only design sample. It does not replace the public home, member dashboard, or current administration screens. The route is intentionally available without sign-in because the preview deployment uses isolated synthetic authentication that cannot accept real L&F passwords.

## Admin visual and usability rules

- Use flat, solid colors with clear contrast. Avoid glossy effects, gradients, and decorative shadows.
- Keep administration compact and scannable. Do not repeat the same metric or action in multiple cards; use direct navigation and grouped sections instead of a card wall.
- Prevent page-level horizontal scrolling at phone, tablet, and desktop widths. Let labels wrap and controls fit their container; if a truly wide data view needs scrolling, contain it within that view.
- Put short, useful instructions beside the fields or choices they explain. Keep the expandable information icon optional for longer explanations, so members can use the form without opening help.
- Keep calendar controls easy to find: master or personal scope, category, ministry, and role filters should combine clearly, and each event should identify approved-role visibility separately from assignment.

All sample dialogs support keyboard use: opening a dialog moves focus into it, Tab stays within it, Escape closes it, and focus returns to the control that opened it. The visitor prayer form requires a separate 18+ affirmation and public-sharing acknowledgement before its sample submission button is enabled.

The app route itself has no L&F or Supabase sign-in requirement. The Vercel project has SSO protection enabled for hosted preview deployments. On Nov. 23, 2026, Chad opened the latest `/rebuild-preview` link in his own browser, reported that it did not ask him to sign in, and confirmed that the design looked good. This clears the visual design review for the preview he saw. A separate cloud-browser session was redirected to Vercel sign-in; access may therefore depend on an existing Vercel session, and other reviewers may still encounter the protection screen.

The preview contains three switchable surfaces:

- Administration: the approved cream sidebar, compact overview, attention queue, schedule, a role-aware master calendar, free event registration and review, an assignment and role-based work queue, a Scripture-centered Studies workspace, a sample People & roles workspace, a sample Prayer review workspace, aggregate Insights, and notification and profile-sharing settings.
- Public homepage: the approved typography-led direction with Luke 19:10 as the focal Scripture. **Join L&F** opens a sample signup dialog with required name, email, password, 18+ affirmation, and guidelines acknowledgement, plus a collapsed optional section for date of birth, phone, gender, ministry interests, referral source, and broad region. DOB is checked for age eligibility if supplied and remains private; optional answers do not affect membership or role approval.
- Member home: personal study continuation and library, a dedicated My groups & ministries workspace, commitments, free registration for an eligible group event, a personal calendar, and a structured request for a private ministry-leader check-in.

The real flow still needs approved retention rules, account-boundary policy, and preflight handling before it can be enabled. The calendar workspace combines master or personal scope with category, ministry, and role filters. Sample entries show which approved roles can see an event and who is assigned; an expandable guide explains that assigned access and approved role access remain distinct. These are presentation samples, not authorization. Visitor submissions are public-only; member privacy is never broadened by moderation. The signup form can be previewed: a valid sample submission explains profile creation and email confirmation, but saves no information and creates no account. The admin Insights workspace shows synthetic aggregate age bands, ministry interests, referral sources, and broad regions only. It does not expose individual answers or exact birth dates; suppression of groups smaller than 10 is a proposal for review. Other preview buttons display explanations. There are no event registration, prayer submission, approval, membership, preference, account, export, deletion, payment, or content writes. The push prompt does not invoke the browser permission API. The client has no API calls or browser storage access. No new dependency or paid service is introduced.

The shared app frame omits its existing header, footer, bottom navigation, floating actions, and push-subscription sync on this exact preview route to avoid duplicate navigation and automatic subscription side effects. Existing routes retain their behavior. The preview is marked noindex.

## Validation

Five focused regression checks cover calendar-scope composition, combined filtering, fixture immutability, public noindex route behavior, and absence of client-side production writes. TypeScript and focused ESLint checks are run separately from the repository's build configuration, which currently ignores TypeScript build errors.

On the unchanged `origin/main` baseline, the full unit suite has eight failures, including a malformed active-guard test and outdated source-string assertions. The preview branch has the same eight failures and adds five passing checks. These baseline failures must not be described as a passing full suite.

Local Chromium failed to launch in this workspace. The public sample was reviewed in a connected browser: Members directory search, My tasks in administration and member views, calendar role filtering, Studies library/group/Emmaus views, Prayer review, People & roles, notifications, and profile sharing fit a 390px viewport without horizontal page overflow. The review confirmed that names not opted into the directory are excluded, selected member fields alone appear, member task queues exclude role approvals, and the existing Emmaus login is noted while study/progress sync remains unimplemented. All data is synthetic. No real browser notification permission was requested, and no production data fixture is included in this change.

The member care check-in and assigned follow-up views remain design prototypes. They do not persist private information, implement real care-role access, or send a message. The calendar view remains a design prototype: it does not create events, configure real sharing, enforce access, or connect to member calendars. Role and group decisions and delegation remain sample-only; privacy sharing persistence, event registration, expanded role catalog, and cross-application progress are not implemented by this visual preview.

The member role-request withdrawal now has an explicit review step naming the exact role and ministry scope. Its confirmation is a sample notice only; it does not withdraw or modify the pending request. Administration role and group decisions use a scoped review panel with an authority acknowledgement, optional decline reason, and sample-only confirmation. Desktop review confirmed that the acknowledgement gates the decision and the sample request stays unchanged. Delegation setup previews reviewer, decision type, exact scope, and end date; desktop review confirmed edits reset the acknowledgement and sample confirmation assigns no permission. Visitor public clearance now requires an explicit acknowledgement before sample confirmation; desktop review confirmed the request remains pending and no content is published.  Responsive review at 390px confirmed no horizontal page overflow in the admin dashboards, member roles, role and group decision panels (including their authority acknowledgement step), scoped delegation, and visitor prayer clearance. At 320px, the delegation form, event planner setup dialog, member event registration dialog, group roster removal review, admin role decision acknowledgement, and member role-request review fit without horizontal page overflow. The registration dialog shows six remaining spaces and keeps Register disabled until the member acknowledges eligibility. The event planner was also reviewed in the connected desktop preview: editing from the review summary returns to the setup form; the selected group and approved-role audiences show their exact scope; the review summary repeats the date, time, location, registration choice, free participation, and adults-only rule; and confirming displays a sample-only notice without creating or sharing an event. Mobile event planner interaction was not fully validated.
