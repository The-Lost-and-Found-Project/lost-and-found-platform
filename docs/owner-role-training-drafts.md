# Owner-assigned role training drafts

Status: multi-session curriculum draft for planning. This material does not record anyone's completion, create a role assignment, or change live permissions. Finalize and version the curriculum, moderation criteria, review checklist, and escalation route before activation. A verifier appointed by Chad or Crissy records completion against the exact version.

Each role has three 60-minute sessions. Sessions use short teaching segments, Scripture reading, participant discussion, and synthetic practice. The facilitator teaches the standard and asks learners to work through the cases; completion is based on demonstrated judgment, not attendance alone.

## Moderator

**Required before activation:** prayer privacy, public moderation, and escalation training.

### Session 1 — Receive prayer with care and protect privacy

**Scripture for reflection:** James 1:19; Proverbs 11:13.

**Outcomes:** the learner can distinguish a visitor submission awaiting public review from a member prayer request; explain the workflow boundary; and identify what information they must not seek or share.

**Session flow**

- 10 min — Read the passages and discuss careful listening, trust, and discretion.
- 15 min — Walk through the public_visitor_prayer_queue and the difference between a visitor's public request and a member-selected private audience.
- 25 min — In pairs, sort synthetic examples into “within assigned queue,” “private/member content,” and “unclear—stop and escalate.”
- 10 min — Each learner explains the boundary in their own words and names the safe next step for a misrouted item.

**Practice:** a pending visitor request; a private member request encountered through an unrelated workflow; and a request whose audience is unclear. Do not use real prayer requests.

### Session 2 — Moderate public submissions consistently

**Scripture for reflection:** James 2:1–4; Ephesians 4:29.

**Outcomes:** the learner can apply the ministry-approved public moderation criteria consistently and choose only an allowed outcome: approve for public display, decline, or hold/escalate.

**Session flow**

- 10 min — Read the passages and discuss impartiality and words that build up.
- 15 min — Review the versioned moderation criteria and examples. If those criteria are not finalized, this session is not sufficient for activation.
- 25 min — Independently classify synthetic cases, then compare reasoning as a group. A case involving uncertainty, private information, or a conflict is held and escalated.
- 10 min — Practice writing a brief, respectful decision reason using an approved reason code. Do not edit the submitter's words or add counseling, diagnosis, or promises on behalf of the ministry.

**Practice:** one straightforward approval, one case that fails the approved criteria, and one ambiguous case that must be held. Use synthetic text only.

### Session 3 — Escalate concerns and preserve accountability

**Scripture for reflection:** Galatians 6:1; Proverbs 15:22.

**Outcomes:** the learner can recognize a conflict or uncertain case, stop without publishing, use the designated escalation route, and record a complete, privacy-safe decision.

**Session flow**

- 10 min — Read the passages and discuss correction with gentleness and seeking wise counsel.
- 15 min — Walk through the hold/escalate path, conflict disclosure, and the limited audit fields used by the approved workflow.
- 25 min — Role-play a conflict-of-interest case, a suspected privacy exposure, and an uncertain submission. Learners practice stopping, escalating, and reporting the event without copying prayer text into unrelated channels.
- 10 min — Verifier asks each learner to explain what the audit record does and does not retain, including the tentative 90-day post-departure retention proposal.

**Practice:** the learner must withhold publication in every case involving a private/member request, audience change, conflict, or uncertainty. No real member data is used.

### Moderator completion evidence

The verifier records the curriculum version, session completion, synthetic case results, decision rationale, privacy-boundary demonstration, and pass/revise outcome. Every privacy, audience, conflict, and uncertainty case must be handled safely. Role activation still requires separate Owner assignment and the approved role authorization flow.

## Content Creator

**Required before activation:** Scripture-first editorial, source/copyright, privacy, and content-review workflow orientation.

### Session 1 — Read Scripture in context

**Scripture for reflection:** 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Luke 24:27.

**Outcomes:** the learner can begin with the passage, identify its literary and historical context at an accessible level, and distinguish biblical text from explanation, interpretation, and application.

**Session flow**

- 10 min — Read the passages and discuss how Scripture forms the foundation of the lesson.
- 15 min — Model observation of a short passage: speaker, audience, setting, movement, repeated ideas, and connection to the surrounding text.
- 25 min — Small groups build a one-page passage map. Any language or translation claim must be tied to how the word is used in context and supported by an identified source, not an isolated dictionary gloss.
- 10 min — Groups share what the passage says before proposing what it means for readers today.

**Practice:** use a passage selected for training and a supplied source packet. Do not reproduce copyrighted source text beyond the permitted use.

### Session 2 — Teach beside the reader

**Scripture for reflection:** Acts 17:11; Colossians 3:16.

**Outcomes:** the learner can write in a clear, narrative-first, teacher-beside-the-reader voice; keep Christ central where the passage leads; and create questions that help a group observe, understand, and respond to Scripture together.

**Session flow**

- 10 min — Read the passages and discuss careful examination and mutual teaching.
- 15 min — Compare a lecture-style explanation with a short guided lesson that invites readers into the text.
- 25 min — In pairs, turn a supplied explanation into a brief teaching segment with open questions, participant work, and an application grounded in the passage.
- 10 min — Peer feedback checks clarity, faithful context, Christ-centered focus, and whether the learners do meaningful work.

**Practice:** create three discussion prompts: one observation question, one meaning question, and one application question. Avoid forcing a connection the passage does not support.

### Session 3 — Build a safe draft and submit it for review

**Scripture for reflection:** 2 Timothy 2:15; Philippians 4:8.

**Outcomes:** the learner can prepare a review-ready draft inside an assigned collection, document sources and permissions, keep member information private, and submit without publishing.

**Session flow**

- 10 min — Read the passages and discuss faithful work and what is true and worthy of attention.
- 15 min — Review the source and copyright checklist, privacy rules, and draft-only permissions.
- 25 min — Create a short synthetic study draft with a passage/topic, audience, learning aim, Scripture observations, discussion/application prompts, and source notes.
- 10 min — Use the review checklist to mark the draft pass/revise and practice responding to feedback with a new version.

**Practice:** use only authorized training materials. Do not include identifiable member prayer, care, or profile information. Do not publish, approve, archive, or edit another creator's work.
