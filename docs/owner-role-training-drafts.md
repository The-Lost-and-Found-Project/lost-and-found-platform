# Owner-assigned role training drafts

Status: draft curriculum outlines for planning. These outlines do not record anyone's completion, create a role assignment, or change live permissions. Finalize and version each curriculum before activation. A verifier appointed by Chad or Crissy records completion against the exact version.

## Moderator

**Required before activation:** prayer privacy, public moderation, and escalation training.

### Learning outline

1. **Privacy and audience boundaries**
   - Distinguish a visitor-submitted public request awaiting review from a member request with a private audience.
   - Review only the assigned `public_visitor_prayer_queue`; do not search for private/member requests or unrelated profile and care information.
   - Never change a member-selected audience or copy, export, screenshot, or share request content outside the authorized workflow.

2. **Consistent public moderation**
   - Apply the ministry's approved privacy and safety criteria consistently to the visitor submission.
   - Record one permitted outcome: approve for public display, decline, or hold/escalate.
   - Use a respectful, non-shaming response where the workflow provides one; do not add pastoral counseling, diagnosis, or promises on behalf of the ministry.
   - Do not edit the submitter's words. A request needing revision is declined or held under the approved workflow.

3. **Escalation and accountability**
   - Recognize a case that needs an Owner or designated safety contact and hold it without publishing.
   - Disclose conflicts and do not review one's own submission.
   - Use only the assigned account and queue; report suspected exposure or misuse promptly and preserve the decision record.

### Demonstration and completion evidence

Use synthetic examples only. The learner classifies sample cases as approve, decline, or hold/escalate and explains the privacy boundary. The verifier checks that every case involving private/member content, an audience change, a conflict, or uncertainty is withheld and escalated appropriately, and that the learner records a clear decision. Do not use real prayer requests in training.

## Content Creator

**Required before activation:** Scripture-first editorial, source/copyright, and content-review workflow orientation.

### Learning outline

1. **Scripture-first study preparation**
   - Keep Scripture as the authority and primary text; preserve passage context and distinguish the biblical text from explanation, application, and interpretation.
   - Identify translation and sources accurately; explain language/context claims with their use in the passage rather than an isolated dictionary gloss.
   - Write for ordinary readers in a clear, narrative-first, teacher-beside-the-reader voice; center the material on Christ and make discussion serve careful engagement with the passage.

2. **Responsible materials and privacy**
   - Create materials only in an explicitly assigned content collection.
   - Use only content and media the ministry is authorized to use; keep source and permission notes with the draft.
   - Do not include identifiable member prayer, care, or profile information without separate documented authorization.

3. **Draft and review workflow**
   - Submit work as a draft with passage/topic, intended audience, learning aim, source notes, and discussion/application prompts.
   - Respond to review comments with a new version and preserve the source trail.
   - Do not publish, approve, archive, or edit another creator's work unless a separate capability and scope are granted.

### Demonstration and completion evidence

The learner prepares a short synthetic study draft from an assigned passage using the review checklist: faithful context, clear distinction between text and explanation, accurate sources, accessible teaching, Christ-centered focus, privacy-safe examples, and usable group discussion. The verifier records the curriculum version and evidence reviewed, provides a pass/revise result, and confirms the learner understands draft-only authority. Do not use copyrighted or member-submitted material unless permission is documented.

## Shared verification record

For either role, the verifier records the learner, role and exact scope, curriculum/evidence version, completion date, verification method, outcome, and verifier identity. Chad or Crissy appoints the verifier or delegates that exact appointment authority. A learner cannot verify their own completion. Training completion does not grant the role, expand scope, or bypass role approval. Until a curriculum is finalized/versioned and the completion is verified, the prerequisite is unmet and the role stays inactive.
